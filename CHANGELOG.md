# Changelog

All notable changes to Nook. Newest first.

## 1.2.1 (2026-11-12)

- **Work with an AI helper.** Claude (through MCP) or your own bot can now read your day and add, move, finish or delete tasks, tick habits, log goals and start a focus session. It only listens on your own PC and needs a secret key that Nook keeps in its data folder. See "Use Nook with Claude" in the README.

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-12)

- **Vision Board**: a new tab for pictures and words of what you are working for. Add by button, drag or Ctrl+V. Link a card to a goal. Press Show for a slow slideshow. Pictures stay on your PC.
- **Goals count themselves.** Pick what moves a goal: all focus time, focus on some tasks, ticked tasks, calendar events with a word, sales calls from Fathom (a no-show does not count), a number that moves (like weight), a list of steps, or just add it yourself.
- Goals: a + button on each goal, weekly or monthly goals that start over, "on track / behind / ahead", a page for each goal with the last 12 weeks, and pause, done and archive.
- **Calendar**: right-click a task for Start, Done, Rename, Move to tomorrow, Move to a date, Duplicate, Remove time and Delete. Right-click a Google event to hide it in Nook. The month view can add tasks.
- No more popups: delete is the x, then the small word Delete. The update notice is one thin line at the bottom.
- The underline when you point at a goal or habit is gone.

## 1.1.5 (2026-11-12)

- Fixed: "Restart now" did not update. The installer saw Nook still closing and gave up.
- Updates are found 5 seconds after Nook opens, then every hour.
- Delete and sign-out now ask in Nook's own popup instead of the browser box.

## 1.1.4 (2026-11-12)

- First public release.
- When an update is ready, Nook asks in a small popup: Later or Restart now.

## 1.1.3 (2026-11-12)

- Google sign-in now uses Nook's own Google app.
- Privacy policy added (PRIVACY.md).

## 1.1.2 (2026-11-12)

- Your own sounds: put any `.wav` file in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FocusOS\sounds` and it shows up in Settings → Sounds. They stay on your PC.

## 1.1.1 (2026-11-12)

- New name: **Nook**, Focus & Productivity Tool (was FocusOS). Existing installs upgrade in place and keep all data.
- Now free for personal and noncommercial use under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-12)

- Change the focus time by scrolling on the ring, or click the time and type it.
- Each task can have its own focus length.
- "Focusing on" box: type any name, or pick from matching tasks.
- 11 soft notification sounds, with a picker for each moment.
- Sign in with any Google account, or use Nook without one.
- Nook now updates itself from GitHub Releases.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-11)

- First Windows app: Today, Calendar, Habits, Goals, Progress, taskbar timer, tray icon, per-user installer.

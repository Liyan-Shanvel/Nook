# Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-11-12

Deepcus is a desktop app for Windows. It runs on your PC. It has no server and no account system of its own.

## What Deepcus stores

Everything you put into Deepcus (tasks, habits, goals, focus sessions, settings) is saved in a file on your own PC, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FocusOS`. It never leaves your PC.

## Google sign-in (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, Deepcus asks for:

- your email address, name and profile picture, to show who is signed in
- read-only access to your Google Calendar, to show your events in Deepcus

Deepcus only reads your calendar. It never creates, changes or deletes events. Your Google data goes straight from Google to your PC, and Deepcus does not send it to the developer or to anyone else. The sign-in token is stored on your PC only.

You can sign out in Deepcus's Settings at any time. You can also remove Deepcus's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deepcus's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Fathom (optional)

If you paste your own Fathom API key in a goal, Deepcus asks Fathom for your recorded calls so a goal can count them. Deepcus keeps only the call title, its date, its length and how many people spoke. It never keeps the recording or what was said. The key and that list stay on your PC. Disconnect removes the key.

## AI helper access

While Deepcus is open it answers requests from programs on your own PC (address 127.0.0.1) that send the secret key in `agent_token.txt`. Web pages are refused. Nothing is opened to the internet. What a helper like Claude does with your data is up to that helper and its own privacy rules.

## Updates

Deepcus checks GitHub Releases for a newer version. That check sends no personal data.

## No tracking

Deepcus has no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

# Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-11-12

Nook is a desktop app for Windows. It runs on your PC. It has no server and no account system of its own.

## What Nook stores

Everything you put into Nook (tasks, habits, goals, focus sessions, settings) is saved in a file on your own PC, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FocusOS`. It never leaves your PC.

## Google sign-in (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, Nook asks for:

- your email address, name and profile picture, to show who is signed in
- read-only access to your Google Calendar, to show your events in Nook

Nook only reads your calendar. It never creates, changes or deletes events. Your Google data goes straight from Google to your PC, and Nook does not send it to the developer or to anyone else. The sign-in token is stored on your PC only.

You can sign out in Nook's Settings at any time. You can also remove Nook's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Nook's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Updates

Nook checks GitHub Releases for a newer version. That check sends no personal data.

## No tracking

Nook has no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
